About Auto-2048

Auto-2048 is a free, open-source 2048 game with a built-in AI solver. Press AI Autoplay to watch an Expectimax search play the game for you, or press Hint to see the best next move. Open AI Settings to tune the search depth and heuristic weights (empty cells, monotonicity, smoothness, corner) and see how they change the AI’s strategy. Source code on GitHub.
